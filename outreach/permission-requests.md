# Data-access permission requests

Drafted 2026-10-29. Each one asks for the written permission its terms require. Fill in the brackets and
send from your own address. Where to send: each site's partnership, legal or contact page; addresses are
not filled in because none were verified. When one says yes, forward the reply: the connector gets built
and the permission is recorded in `sources.yaml` (`access_check`).

What every email promises (keep it true): titles, short summaries and a link back; no personal data
(no names, profiles, emails); deletions honored; one request per second at most; attribution shown.
